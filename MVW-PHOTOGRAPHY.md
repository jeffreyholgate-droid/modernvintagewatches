# MVW Photography Standard

## Porcelain Studio

Use a square studio frame across the catalogue. The ivory backdrop runs diagonally from `#F9F6EF` at the upper left to `#E9E1D4` at the lower right. The contact shadow uses `#4C3D32` at low opacity. Paint the shadow beneath the watch layer. Skip reflections and background props. For new photography use broad, soft light from the upper left, and watch for bright reflections that conceal wear.

Centre the retained subject. Its longest dimension should occupy about 83% of the square. Preserve proportions. Vary the hero angle to bring out the individual watch, then include a straight-on dial view. Use the same backdrop and subject scale for vintage and modern watches. Check that the dial and silhouette read at a 160 px square thumbnail.

Aim for six distinct views where the source supports them: hero, straight-on dial, crown side, caseback, clasp or strap, and notable wear. Add a separate photo for box and papers when relevant. Check paperwork and accessories for information that should not be displayed.

## Prepare a listing

1. Confirm permission to use the exact source photos. Archive the unedited originals in a private folder under the listing ID with the source and retrieval date. Store exports separately.
2. Review the actual photos of the watch offered for sale. For a hero image, require a complete silhouette, legible dial and clear case edges. If a hand hides the bracelet, a crop cuts off the subject, or glare hides condition, obtain a better photo. A properly labelled detail shot can be tight.
3. In **Admin → Inventory → Photo review**, choose the cover, outline the visible watch and relevant accessories, then compare the result with its original. Trace any openings where the backdrop should show through. A hand or stand can be removed from the setting only where it does not overlap the subject.
4. Start with original lighting. Use a small whole-image tonal adjustment only when it improves legibility and keeps colours and wear faithful. Compare at full size and as a thumbnail. Check the dial text, indices, hands, patina, scratches, dents, finishing, case edges and strap or bracelet. If any detail is less visible, restore the original lighting or request a better source.
5. Approve each image before publishing. Keep the original available on the product page. If the seller changes the source gallery, compare the replacement photos and reapprove the full set.

## Files

- For a static export use a 1,600 × 1,600 px square sRGB master when the source supports it. Export WebP for the catalogue and JPEG when needed for sharing. Start with a 250–450 KB target per hero, then check dial lettering and surface detail at full size.
- Use `mvw-reference-angle-v01.webp`. Add the listing ID where references repeat. Keep separate versioned exports for a 4:5 social crop if the full watch still fits.
- The app stores the outline and lighting treatment separately from the source reference, then renders the composite non-destructively. It does not archive provider-hosted originals or generate static WebP files. Maintain the private archive for owned or authorised source photos.

## Publication check

Use only pixels from the actual watch. No retouching of wear, reconstruction of concealed parts, stock substitutes or generated watches. Retain the precise outline without posterised edges or leftover background. Review the original side by side and hold any photo that cannot meet this standard.
